Professional Space Planning

Space planning is one of the most important foundations of interior design. Before selecting colors, furniture, or decorations, we study how people will move, work, relax, interact, and live inside the space.

A successful space plan considers circulation, furniture placement, room proportions, storage, accessibility, natural light, artificial lighting, and the relationship between different areas.

Our Space Planning Process Includes:

  • Understanding the client's requirements
  • Measuring the available space
  • Studying the existing layout
  • Analyzing movement and circulation
  • Planning functional zones
  • Determining furniture placement
  • Creating efficient storage solutions
  • Considering natural lighting
  • Planning electrical and lighting positions
  • Improving accessibility and comfort
  • Balancing open and private spaces
  • Making the best use of every available area
“Good space planning turns empty square footage into meaningful living.”
“Every corner has potential when it is designed with purpose.”

Our Interior Design Process

01. Consultation

We begin by understanding your ideas, requirements, lifestyle, preferences, budget, and expectations.

02. Site Analysis

We study the space, measurements, structure, natural lighting, circulation, and existing conditions.

03. Space Planning

We create a functional layout that makes efficient use of the available space.

04. Design Concept

We develop the visual direction of the project, including style, mood, colors, materials, textures, and design language.

05. Furniture & Material Selection

We select furniture, finishes, fabrics, lighting, and materials that support the overall design concept.

06. Detailed Planning

Detailed drawings and planning are prepared to guide the implementation of the design.

07. Execution

The design begins to become reality through careful coordination, craftsmanship, and attention to detail.

08. Final Styling

The final details, decorations, accessories, and finishing touches are added to complete the space.
