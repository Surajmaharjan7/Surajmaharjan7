Meet Suraj Maharjan

Founder of Vesun Interior and passionate interior designer, creating spaces where beauty and functionality work together.

Who I Am

My name is Suraj Maharjan. I am the founder of Vesun Interior and an interior designer passionate about transforming ordinary spaces into beautiful experiences.


I believe that every space has potential. With thoughtful planning, creative ideas, proper materials, lighting, furniture and details, a simple room can become something truly special.

My Design Philosophy

Interior design should not only look beautiful. It should improve the way people live, work, relax and experience their everyday environment.


Every client is different. Every lifestyle is different. That is why every space should have its own identity.
